## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors to a TEKGUYZ demo: owners of roofing companies (long quotes, storm surges) and HVAC and AC companies (system replacements). The owner watches one board; in the demo the visitor can also play the customer who gets the texts.

## Product Purpose

A landing page and a one-click demo of quote and invoice follow-up by text. Line: "No quote or invoice goes quiet." Success: an owner sees that every quote and unpaid invoice gets followed up politely until the customer answers or pays, without anyone remembering to do it.

## Positioning

Persistent, never pushy: capped, polite follow-ups that stop the moment the customer answers or pays, and hand the last step to the owner as a call.

## Operating Context

The demo runs as Gumbo Limbo Air, a made-up AC and heating company in South Florida. The visitor presses Skip ahead a day and watches follow-ups go out, customers answer, jobs get invoiced and invoices get paid. Everything stays in the visitor's browser.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- At most three follow-ups on a quote and three reminders on an invoice; quiet on Sundays; after the last one the owner is asked to call.
- The invoice goes out by itself when the crew marks the job done.
- Never sends or charges anything in the demo; nothing is kept on a server.

## Brand Commitments

- The shipped look in `DESIGN.md` is binding. This is a shipped demo: never restyle it or roll a new visual world unless the founder names this demo and asks for that (`claude-config/LOOKS.md`, rule 1).
- Fit the app's job, not the customer's trade: money and trust, so steady, clear and readable; never hazard yellow, steel grey or job-site looks (`LOOKS.md`, "Fit the app").
- Say: "No quote or invoice goes quiet." "Persistent. Never pushy." Never say it nags, or that anything is sent or charged, or that Gumbo Limbo Air is real.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. Never show a real business, a TEKGUYZ price, a statistic or a phone number.

## Product Principles

- One board answers: what is waiting, who answered, who to call, what got paid.
- The rules are simple enough to say out loud.
- Honest about the demo: every simulated step says so.
